🛡️ Downloading LLOOP.exe Guidelines
1. If Chrome/Edge flags the download:
Click the download icon in your browser top-right ➔ click Keep / Keep anyway.
2. If Windows SmartScreen appears:
Click More info ➔ click Run anyway. *(Occurs once for new apps)*.

🚀 How to Use (3 Simple Steps)

Step 1

Download & Open

Download LLOOP.exe and double-click to open. No installation wizard or administrator rights required.

Step 2

Enter Your Local Port

Type your local web app's port number (e.g. 3000, 5000, or 8000).

Step 3

Share Your Live HTTPS Link

Click Start Tunnel. Copy your secure public HTTPS URL or scan the mobile QR code on your phone!
