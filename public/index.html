Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sharing local ports securely.

What is SHARE PORT?

SHARE PORT is a zero-configuration Windows desktop application that lets developers expose any local port (such as 3000, 5000, or 8000) to the internet over a secure, publicly accessible HTTPS URL in just one click.

How do I share my local port over the internet?

Simply launch SHARE PORT, enter the port number where your local web application is running, and click Start Tunnel. Your secure public HTTPS address is created instantly and ready to share.

Do I need router port forwarding or firewall configuration?

No. SHARE PORT automatically traverses NAT and firewalls. You do not need to log into your router, configure port forwarding rules, or manage static IP addresses.

Can I test webhooks with SHARE PORT?

Yes. SHARE PORT provides real SSL-certified HTTPS URLs that work seamlessly with webhooks from payment gateways (Stripe, Razorpay, PayPal), authentication providers, and cloud services, complete with a built-in traffic inspector to examine payloads.

Does SHARE PORT require creating an account or signing in?

No account or login is required. You can download and run SHARE PORT immediately without signing up, entering credit cards, or generating complex auth tokens.

Which web frameworks and languages are supported?

SHARE PORT works with any HTTP/HTTPS server or framework including Node.js, React, Vite, Next.js, Express, Python (FastAPI, Flask, Django), Go, PHP, Ruby on Rails, .NET, Spring Boot, and Docker containers.
