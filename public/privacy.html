Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026  •  Product: SHARE PORT  •  Version: v1.0.30

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how SHARE PORT ("we", "our", or "the Application") respects and protects developer privacy when using the SHARE PORT Windows desktop application and official website (https://www.shareport.in).

SHARE PORT is a zero-configuration developer utility designed to share local development servers via temporary, encrypted public HTTPS URLs without requiring user registration, user accounts, or personal data collection.

2. Information Collection & Zero-Tracking Guarantee

SHARE PORT does not collect, store, track, sell, or profile any personal data.

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, accounts, or payment details.
  • Application Data & Local Code: SHARE PORT never collects, inspects, stores, or logs your local source code, database contents, credentials, or private application payloads.
  • Local Device Storage: Any app configuration settings (such as chosen default port or preferences) remain stored strictly on your local machine.

💡 Summary: SHARE PORT runs as a client on your machine. Your data, code, and payloads remain strictly private within your environment.

3. Data Transmission & Security

All temporary public URLs generated by SHARE PORT utilize standard, end-to-end TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit. Tunnel connections are isolated, temporary, and terminate immediately when you stop the session or exit the application.

4. Children's Privacy

SHARE PORT is a developer utility and does not knowingly target, solicit, or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements. Any revisions will be published directly on this page (https://www.shareport.in/privacy) with an updated effective date.
