Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026  |  Product: SHARE PORT

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how SHARE PORT ("we", "our", or "the Application") respects and protects user privacy when using the SHARE PORT application and website (https://www.shareport.in).

SHARE PORT is a developer utility designed to share local development web servers via secure temporary HTTPS URLs without requiring user registration, accounts, or personal data submission.

2. Information Collection & Data Privacy

SHARE PORT does not collect, store, track, or share any personal data.

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, accounts, or payment information.
  • Application Data & Local Code: SHARE PORT does not collect, inspect, store, or log your local source code, database contents, or application request payloads.
  • Local Device Storage: Any app configuration settings remain stored strictly on your local machine.

💡 Summary: SHARE PORT runs locally on your machine. Your data remains private to your environment.

3. Data Security

All temporary URLs generated by SHARE PORT utilize standard TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit. Tunnels terminate automatically when you stop the session or close the application.

4. Children's Privacy

SHARE PORT does not knowingly target or collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published directly on this page (https://www.shareport.in/privacy) with an updated effective date.
