Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026  |  Product: SHARE PORT

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SHARE PORT ("we", "our", or "the App") handles user privacy and information when using the SHARE PORT desktop application and website (https://www.shareport.in).

SHARE PORT is designed as a developer utility tool that allows developers to expose local development web servers to secure, temporary public HTTPS URLs without registering accounts or installing complex software. Respecting your privacy and securing your local data is fundamental to our design.

2. Information Collection & Usage

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): SHARE PORT does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP tracking databases, user accounts, or financial payment information.
  • Tunnel & HTTP Traffic Data: When SHARE PORT establishes a local proxy tunnel to share your localhost port, HTTP/HTTPS request traffic flows between your local server and temporary tunnel endpoints. SHARE PORT does not log, store, inspect, or retain your application data payloads, headers, or request content on any remote servers.
  • Local Application Configuration: Any configuration settings (such as local port numbers or UI preferences) are stored strictly on your local device.

💡 Key Takeaway: SHARE PORT operates entirely locally on your machine. Your source code, local database content, and user secrets remain strictly on your local environment.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

SHARE PORT interacts with trusted third-party infrastructure solely to deliver software binary downloads and encrypted proxy tunnel connectivity:

  • Cloudflare Tunnels: Temporary HTTPS URLs are powered by Cloudflare Tunnel infrastructure to provide TLS-encrypted transit between your client and your localhost. Traffic passing through Cloudflare is subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Releases: Executable application downloads (.exe) are hosted via GitHub Releases. Interactions with GitHub are governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Vercel Edge Network: The official site (www.shareport.in) is hosted on Vercel. Static page requests are subject to Vercel's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security

All public URLs created by SHARE PORT use standard HTTPS encryption (TLS/SSL). Tunnels automatically terminate when you close the SHARE PORT application or click "Stop Tunnel", immediately disabling the temporary public route to your local port.

5. Children's Privacy

SHARE PORT is a developer utility tool and does not direct services to or knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect policy requirements or application enhancements. Any changes will be published directly on this page (https://www.shareport.in/privacy.html) with an updated effective date.
